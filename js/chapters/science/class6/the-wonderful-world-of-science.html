What is Science?

Science is a way of thinking, observing, asking questions and testing ideas to understand the world. It is not only a list of facts. Curiosity starts the investigation; evidence helps us decide whether an idea works.

Scientist's rule: A guess is useful only when we are willing to test it.

The Scientific Method

1

Observe

Notice something interesting, surprising or not understood.

2

Ask

Turn your curiosity into a clear question.

3

Hypothesise

Suggest a possible explanation that can be tested.

4

Test

Change a factor deliberately and collect observations.

5

Analyse & conclude

Compare the evidence with your prediction. If it does not fit, improve the question or hypothesis and test again.

🎯 Young Scientist Mission

You are given a mystery: a classroom plant is weak and has yellow leaves. You may investigate light, water and soil nutrients. Your job is not to guess the answer—it is to design a fair test.

  1. Choose one factor to change.
  2. Keep the other factors as constant as possible.
  3. Predict the result.
  4. Run the test and record observations.
  5. Explain what the evidence supports.

🧪 Fair-test checklist

  • Change one main variable at a time.
  • Use repeated trials when possible.
  • Measure or record observations instead of relying only on memory.
  • Do not force the conclusion to match your prediction.
